02 — The Story

Why we started

The missing layer:
verified work rails.

Our founder saw a repeatable pattern: capable people in secondary cities with no portable proof-of-work — filtered out by credential-centric platforms built for capital hubs.

Gig marketplaces require existing portfolios. National job boards optimize for pedigree. Public programs often leave no durable hireable record.

GigCareer exists to own the verification layer: local projects → LER records → skills-based matching. Korea is the pilot; the model is region-agnostic.

We connect workforce agencies, enterprises, and capital around a shared data standard for employability.
